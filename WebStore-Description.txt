Quickly convert geographic coordinates (latitude and longitude) into Google Maps Plus Codes with zero friction!

With Copy Google Maps Plus Code, boost your daily workflow: just copy coordinates to your clipboard and click the extension icon. The conversion is processed offline instantly, and the resulting Plus Code is copied right back to your clipboard with an on-screen toast confirmation.

- Full Multilingual Support: Ready in English, Portuguese, Spanish, and French.
- 100% Offline Processing: Powered by the official OpenLocationCode library directly in your browser without external servers.

- Calculations run purely locally on your device.

How to Use:
1. Copy GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude) to your clipboard.
2. Click the extension icon in your Chrome toolbar.
3. Paste your Plus Code anywhere!

2.2 TECHNICAL PERMISSION JUSTIFICATIONS (ALWAYS IN ENGLISH)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• activeTab:
Required to display a temporary, non-intrusive on-screen toast confirmation directly on the user's currently focused tab when the extension action is triggered.

• scripting:
Required to execute the lightweight conversion script upon the user's click, validating the clipboard coordinates, running the local OpenLocationCode algorithm, and injecting the confirmation toast element.

• clipboardRead:
Strictly required to read the latitude and longitude coordinates that the user previously copied to the system clipboard, triggered only when clicking the extension icon. No other clipboard contents are ever read, transmitted, or stored.

• clipboardWrite:
Required to automatically write the generated Google Maps Plus Code back to the user's system clipboard so it can be pasted immediately anywhere.

• host_permissions (<all_urls>):
Required on-demand so the extension can show the confirmation toast notification overlay on whichever active webpage the user happens to be browsing when clicking the action button.
